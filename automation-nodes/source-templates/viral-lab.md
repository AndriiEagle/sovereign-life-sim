# Viral Lab Reference

Viral Lab is the default operating mode for Brand Social OS. It favors fast, real experiments over polished but slow campaigns.

## Weekly Loop

1. Pick one growth thesis.
2. Generate 7 daily core ideas.
3. Adapt each idea across TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, X, Threads, LinkedIn, and Reddit.
4. Export everything to approval status.
5. Publish only approved items through Postiz or OmniSocials.
6. Log metrics after 24h, 72h, and 7d.
7. Double down on winners and retire weak formats.

## Content Pillars

- Build in public: show real progress, artifacts, mistakes, and decisions.
- Social series: recurring episodes with a clear promise and unresolved tension.
- Useful templates: turn learnings into prompts, checklists, scripts, or workflows.
- Metric autopsies: explain why a post won, stalled, or failed.
- Audience-to-product: convert repeated comments into product hypotheses.

## Scoring

Prioritize posts that create saves, shares, comments, follows, and qualified clicks. Views matter only when they create repeatable behavior or audience memory.

Suggested weighted score:

```text
score = likes + comments*2 + shares*3 + saves*4 + follows*6 + clicks*4
```

## Platform Notes

- TikTok, Reels, Shorts: conflict-first hook, vertical video, fast scene changes, captions, one idea.
- X and Threads: one sharp thesis, proof, lesson, and reply-bait through genuine questions only.
- LinkedIn: practical operator framing, specific result, and a clear takeaway.
- Reddit: community-native post with context, no disguised selling, and useful detail.
